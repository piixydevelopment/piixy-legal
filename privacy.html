Piixy — Privacy Policy

Última actualización: 23 de noviembre de 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Piixy handles information when users interact with the bot on Discord.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features enabled, Piixy may process:

  • Discord user IDs.
  • Discord server IDs.
  • Virtual currency balances (PXcoins).
  • User profiles, statistics and activity related to bot features.
  • Virtual shop purchases and active item effects.
  • Information necessary to operate and maintain the bot.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Operate the virtual economy and PXcoins system.
  • Maintain user profiles and statistics.
  • Process shop purchases and item effects.
  • Provide minigames and interactive features.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse.
  • Improve the reliability of Piixy.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in databases required for Piixy's operation. Retention periods depend on the type of information and operational requirements.

5. Data Sharing

Piixy does not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure or hosting providers when necessary to operate the service, subject to applicable safeguards.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information. However, no digital system can guarantee absolute security.

7. User Requests

Users may contact the Piixy development team through its official support channels to ask questions or request information about their stored data, including deletion where applicable.

8. Discord

Piixy operates on Discord and is subject to Discord's platform policies and privacy practices. Users should also review Discord's Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in Piixy's functionality or data practices. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the Piixy development team through its official support channels.
